TFM11 practice test

Fire Alarm Statutes & Rules. Twenty free questions on Texas Insurance Code Chapter 6002 and Texas Administrative Code 34.600 — which, per the State Fire Marshal’s Office, is the entire basis of this exam.

What TFM11 actually tests

There is no NFPA 72 on this exam and no electrical theory. It is a statute and rule exam: who must be licensed, who is exempt, what a registered firm must do, how renewal and continuing education work, what the SFMO may discipline you for, and which acts are criminal offences.

It is 50 questions, true/false or multiple choice, 70% to pass, closed book, through PSI. Because it is pure recall of a specific statute, it is the more reliably passable of the two exams — repetition genuinely works here in a way it does not on the technical exam.

Every question in this test cites the statute it comes from. All 20 carry the Texas Insurance Code section, checked against the full current text of Chapter 6002 as published by the Texas Legislature's own statute service — not a secondary copy. In the full bank, 99 of 123 TFM11 questions carry a section citation. The remaining 24 rest on rules in 28 TAC Chapter 34 rather than on the statute, and are left uncited rather than given a guessed rule number.

The most common failure on TFM11 is studying it with TFM12. Mixing a statute exam and a code exam into one study session means the statute material never gets the repetition it rewards. Drill this one on its own until the exemptions and the renewal timing are automatic.
