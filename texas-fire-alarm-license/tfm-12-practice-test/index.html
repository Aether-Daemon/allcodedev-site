TFM12 practice test

Fire Alarm Technician — the technical exam. Twenty free questions on NFPA 72 and NEC Article 760 with worked explanations; most name the section they come from, and every section number printed was checked against the edition Texas adopted.

What TFM12 actually tests

Per the SFMO, this exam is built on the fire alarm sections of the adopted NFPA standards — including NFPA 72 except the Household Fire Alarm Systems chapter, which is TFM13’s exam, not this one. The Technician Qualifying portion also covers NEC Articles 760 and 300, plus NFPA 13, 17, 17A, 25 and 90A.

It is closed book. That changes how you study: you are not learning to look a requirement up, you are learning where requirements live and what the numbers are.

Texas tests NFPA 72-2019, adopted through 28 TAC §34.607 in March 2023 — not the current 2025 edition. Section numbers moved between editions, so a study guide citing the 2013 or 2022 text will hand you numbers that do not exist in the tested edition. Confirm with your local AHJ too; local adoption can differ from the state rule.

Where candidates lose points

  • PLFA vs NPLFA. Two power sources, two sets of rules, and distractors built from the other one. NEC 760.2 defines both by which section the source complies with.
  • Circuit integrity and survivability. CI cable, the pathway survivability levels, and what each one actually requires.
  • The arithmetic. Ohm’s law, voltage drop, and secondary power calculations are not optional and cannot be looked up.
  • Chapter 14 testing frequencies. Easy marks if you have drilled the table, guesses if you have not.
